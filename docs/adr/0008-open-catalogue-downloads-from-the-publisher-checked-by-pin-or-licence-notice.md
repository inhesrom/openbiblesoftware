# The Open catalogue downloads from the publisher, checked by a pin or by the licence notice

Get Resources lists openly licensed Resources that are not SWORD modules from a compiled-in data file, `crates/engine/data/open-catalogue.toml` (#134). Each entry names the publisher's own URL, the importer that reads it, its licence (SPDX and licence class), its Attribution and changes, and archived licence evidence: an evidence file in `crates/engine/data/open-evidence/` and an Internet Archive capture of the publisher's licence page. A download is never re-hosted or mirrored by us. Before it is imported it is checked in one of two ways. If the entry pins a sha256 (the publisher's files are stable), the download must match it. If the entry names a licence file (the publisher regenerates its files, as eBible.org does with every zip on every build), the licence notice inside the download, read as text up to eBible's build stamp, must have the sha256 the entry pins, and the entry's evidence file quotes that notice whole. The notice is never read for its meaning: any difference refuses the download. Earlier versions of #134 read the notice for a phrase and for negations or restrictions that take it back; five review rounds each found a wording that got past (a negation in another sentence, an abbreviated or spelled-out restriction, a non-breaking hyphen), so the check fails closed instead. The same goes for SWORD's `DistributionLicense`: a Licence class comes only from two allowlists of whole values seen in the live catalogs, and an Open catalogue entry declares its class beside its evidence. A download that fails the check is refused and leaves nothing in the Library. Only Free to share works are listed (Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, GPL), and no file comes from ccel.org (#20).

## Considered Options

- **Pin every entry through an Internet Archive capture, as the Starter library does:** the bytes are fixed and can be reviewed, but every download would come from the Internet Archive, not the publisher. The ticket asks for the publisher's own URL, and an archive capture also goes stale when the publisher corrects the text.
- **Download from the publisher, unpinned, with no check:** simple, but if a publisher changed its licence, the app would import the new edition under the old licence and Attribution.
- **Download from the publisher; check the licence notice for a phrase and for wording that takes it back:** tried first. Every review found a new wording that got past, so free text is not read for its meaning.
- **Download from the publisher; pin if the files are stable, else pin the licence notice in the download (chosen):** users get the publisher's current edition. The licence the app records is checked against the file actually imported, and the archived evidence shows what the licence was when the entry was added.
- **Generate entries from eBible's `translations.csv`:** over a thousand Bibles at once, but the CSV has no licence column (only `Redistributable` and a free-text `Copyright`), so there would be no per-entry evidence.

## Consequences

- Adding an entry is a reviewed data-file change with its evidence file. The `open_catalogue` tests fail if an entry is not Free to share, its class does not match its SPDX id, its evidence is missing or does not cite the archived page, it names a ccel.org URL, or it is checked by neither a pin nor a pinned licence notice, or its evidence does not quote the pinned notice.
- An unpinned entry's sha256 is recorded in the Import record (`OpenOrigin::sha256`), so the edition a user has can still be identified afterwards.
- An eBible entry stops importing if eBible changes anything in the notice part of `copr.htm`, even a date or a word's case. That is intended: the entry must then be reviewed and re-pinned, with its evidence quoting the new notice.
- Resources downloaded from the Open catalogue are ordinary imported Resources (`Origin::Imported`). Their manifest carries the entry's licence, Attribution and changes, so the About view and Help → Credits (Downloaded open Resources) show them with no extra data.
