# "Open Bible" name availability (#328 spike)

Can the planned iOS and Android apps be called **Open Bible** (full name **Open Bible Software**),
and what iOS bundle id and Android package name should they take? Store listings, trademark
records, store naming rules and identifier rules were checked live from this project's research
host on 2026-11-10 (the USPTO TTABVUE page fetched that day stamps "Results as of 11/10/2026").
Live store *search* was not available: listings were found through web search and then each
listing page was opened directly, so a store may hold more "Open Bible" titles than are listed
here. This note is research, not legal advice; the trademark section records what the public
registers show.

What the history shows:

- When the churches applied for OPEN BIBLE, the examiner refused it under §2(d) (likelihood of
  confusion), citing THE OPEN BIBLE and THE NEW OPEN BIBLE, and required a disclaimer of BIBLE.
  The TTAB affirmed "as to disclaimer requirement only", so the confusion refusal did not
  stand for a church-membership mark. [USPTO OG 2004](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/com/sol/og/2004/week44/pattab12.htm)
- Thomas Nelson then opposed the churches' mark (Opposition 91165099, filed 2005-04-28), with
  settlement suspensions, a withdrawal on 2006-08-16 and dismissal with prejudice. The owner of
  THE OPEN BIBLE has taken action over the words "Open Bible" before.
  [TTABVUE](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/v?pno=91165099&pty=OPP)
- Thomas Nelson still sells *The Open Bible* (NKJV study Bible; new edition listed Jan 28 2025;
  topical index, cross-references, maps). [Thomas Nelson product page](https://www.thomasnelsonbibles.com/product/the-open-bible-nkjv/)
  (the page returned 403 to the fetcher; the description comes from the search-result summary)
  and [Google Books record](https://books.google.com/books?id=VpWq0AEACAAJ). A study app that
  delivers Bibles plus cross-references sells into the same market as a printed study Bible
  with the same name. That is the closest overlap found.

## OpenBible.info

- OpenBible.info ("Remix Bible data") publishes the geocoding and the 340,000 cross-references
  the app ships. Its content is CC BY 4.0 "unless otherwise indicated". The site's only contact
  is "openbibleinfo (at) gmail.com". [OpenBible.info](https://www.openbible.info/),
  [Cross references](https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/)
- CC BY 4.0 grants **no trademark rights** (§2(b)(2)). Its "No endorsement" clause (§2(a)(6))
  gives no permission "to assert or imply that You are … connected with, or sponsored, endorsed,
  or granted official status by, the Licensor". [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en)
- No trademark filing for "OpenBible.info" turned up (this was not a full USPTO search).
- Risk: the Credits name "OpenBible.info" on the same screens as an app called "Open Bible", so
  a reader may think OpenBible.info made or endorses the app. A third-party Android app built on
  OpenBible.info data has already used the name ("OpenBible.info Topical Bible",
  `com.caseybrooks.openbible`, above). The licence does not forbid the name. It does forbid
  implying endorsement, so the attribution text has to say the data is used, not partnered.
- A different site, **openbible.com**, offers mostly CC0 content and Bible texts with notices.
  Its operator was not confirmed. [openbible.com permissions](https://openbible.com/download.htm)

## Store naming rules

| Rule | Apple App Store | Google Play |
|---|---|---|
| Name length | 2–30 characters ([App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/); [Guideline 2.3.7](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)) | ≤ 30 characters, full- or half-width ([Create and set up your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en); [Metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en)) |
| Uniqueness | "You can use an app name for one app per localization." A trademark owner can file a claim against another developer's name ([Add a new app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app/)). Guideline 2.3.7: "Choose a unique app name" | No uniqueness rule in the policy. Two live Play apps are titled "Open Bible" / "open bible" (table above) |
| Changing it later | Editable until submission; afterwards with a new version ([App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/)). Removing an app gives up the name ([Remove an app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/remove-an-app)) | Store listing field, one localized name per language ([Create and set up your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en)) |
| Second line | **Subtitle**, ≤ 30 characters, shown under the name "throughout the App Store". Apple suggests the subtitle, "rather than your app's name", for explaining the value; it changes with a new version ([Product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)) | **Short description**, ≤ 80 characters ([Create and set up your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en)) |
| Content limits | "Be distinctive. Avoid names that use generic terms or are too similar to existing app names" ([Product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)). 5.2.1: no third-party trademarks without permission and no "copycat … names". 4.1(a): don't "make some minor changes to another app's name" ([Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). Keywords: no "competing app names" or unauthorised trademarks ([Product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)) | No emoji, no ALL CAPS unless part of the brand, no ranking/price/promo words ("free", "#1"), no misleading metadata; "Don't use a celebrity's name or a brand's logo without permission" ([Metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en)) |
| Home-screen label | `CFBundleDisplayName`, "the user-visible name for the bundle … visible on the iOS Home screen", set in the app bundle, not in the store record ([CFBundleDisplayName](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/cfbundledisplayname.md)) | Manifest `android:label` (also set in the app) |

Apple consequences:

- The exact English store name **"Open Bible" is taken** on the US storefront (Conrad Bate,
  `id6754892616`). Under the one-name-per-localization rule we cannot take it unless that app is
  removed or we have trademark rights to claim it, and we have none.
- **"Open Bible Software"** is 19 characters and was not found as an existing title. Whether
  Apple's uniqueness check counts it as "too similar" is unknown until an app record is created
  (see Gaps).
- The home-screen label can still read "Open Bible": no uniqueness rule for it was found in
  Apple's docs. Guideline 5.2.1 ("copycat … names … in your app bundle") still applies to it.
- Free, ad-free and similar promotional words must not go in the name. They also cannot go in
  the subtitle: Apple asks for features and typical uses there, not slogans.

## Identifiers when the project owns no domain

Format rules (the id must satisfy both platforms if one id is shared):

| | iOS bundle id | Android applicationId |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | A–Z a–z 0–9, `-`, `.`; reverse-DNS "typically"; case-insensitive ([CFBundleIdentifier](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/cfbundleidentifier.md)) | ≥ 2 segments, each starts with a letter, `[a-zA-Z0-9_]` only ([Configure the app module](https://developer.android.com/build/configure-app-module)) |
| Shared safe subset | lowercase letters, digits, dots; **no `-`** (illegal on Android) and **no `_`** (illegal on iOS) | |

Precedent for `io.github.<user>.<app>`:

- Komelia ships on Google Play and F-Droid as `io.github.snd_r.komelia`.
  [Komelia README](https://github.com/Snd-R/Komelia)
- F-Droid forum moderators point GitHub-hosted apps to `io.github…` ids, citing F-Droid's
  inclusion policy (the policy itself was not opened).
  [F-Droid forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/t/do-i-need-to-change-the-app-id-to-upload-my-app-to-f-droid/19424)
- Flathub checks `io.github.*` / `com.github.*` ids by logging in to the matching GitHub account:
  the third id segment must be the GitHub user ([verification.py](https://github.com/flathub-infra/website/blob/main/backend/app/verification.py),
  [Flathub verification](https://docs.flathub.org/docs/for-app-authors/verification)).
  `io.github.inhesrom.*` therefore stays verifiable without a domain, but only through the
  `inhesrom` account.
- Flathub ids are "constant"; renaming one means resubmitting the app.
  [Flathub requirements](https://docs.flathub.org/docs/for-app-authors/requirements)

Neither store requires the id to match a domain the developer owns: the reverse-DNS form is a
convention ([Android Developers Blog, "Things That Cannot Change"](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2011/06/things-that-cannot-change.html)).
There is one domain tie, on Android: **organisation** accounts under Android developer
verification must verify a website through Google Search Console. Individual accounts are not
described as needing one. [Android developer verification](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides)

Candidate: **`io.github.inhesrom.openbible`** on both platforms. It is valid on both (letters
and dots only), free of collisions with `com.openbibleapp` / `com.openbible.app`, verifiable
through GitHub, and shareable with a future desktop Flatpak/macOS id. If the repo later moves to
a GitHub organisation, the id keeps naming `inhesrom`. That is cosmetic in the stores, but
Flathub verification stays tied to that account.

## What can never change after first publish

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| iOS bundle id | Can't change after a build is uploaded. The explicit App ID can't be deleted, and after removing the app the bundle id can't be reused | [CFBundleIdentifier](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/cfbundleidentifier.md); [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/); [Remove an app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/remove-an-app) |
| iOS SKU | Can't change after the app is added; can't be reused in the organization after removal | [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/); [Remove an app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/remove-an-app) |
| iOS app name | Changeable with a new version, but removing the app gives it up | same |
| Android applicationId | "Don't change the application ID after you publish"; a changed id is a new app | [Configure the app module](https://developer.android.com/build/configure-app-module) |
| Play package name | "unique and permanent … can't be deleted or re-used" | [Create and set up your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en) |
| Android signing key | "the signing key never changes during the lifetime of your app"; updates install only if certificates match. Play App Signing can upgrade the key, but the new key signs only for Android 13+ and the old key keeps signing for earlier versions | [Sign your app](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing) |
| Play-vs-GitHub key | With Play App Signing, Google signs what users install. A GitHub APK signed with our own key can't update a Play install or the reverse, unless we give Google our own app signing key ("Export and upload") on the first release | [Sign your app](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing) |
| Package registration | Android developer verification links a package name to a verified identity and signing key. Enforcement starts 2026-09-30 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand, globally in 2027, on certified devices "regardless of your app's download source". The sideloaded GitHub APK needs registration (Play Console handles apps on and off Play), or users must take the "advanced flow" | [Android developer verification](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides); [Registering package names](https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16640821?hl=en) |
| Package name, first come | For a new package name with under 50 installs, registration is first-come, first-served; anyone else must request it | [Registering package names](https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16640821?hl=en) |

## Implications for Open Bible mobile

1. **The bare store name "Open Bible" is not available on the App Store** (English localization
   held by the OTB app). On Play it is technically allowed but would be the third "Open Bible"
   there and would sit next to an app with the same name and purpose. Use **"Open Bible Software"**
   (19 chars) as the store name on both stores. Use the subtitle and short description for
   function, e.g. "Offline Bible study & notes" (27 chars). Keep "Open Bible" as the
   home-screen label only if the trademark review below allows it.
2. **Trademark risk is real, not theoretical.** THE OPEN BIBLE (Thomas Nelson/HarperCollins)
   is a live registration for a study Bible with cross-references, and its owner opposed an
   "OPEN BIBLE" filing in 2005. BIBLE is descriptive, which makes "OPEN" carry the mark. A Bible
   study app is close to printed Bibles, and "Software" is also descriptive, so adding it may not
   remove the conflict. This needs a trademark lawyer's clearance opinion (US + EU) before the name
   is fixed. Have a fallback name ready in case.
3. **OpenBible.info:** keep the attribution wording plainly "data from OpenBible.info, CC BY
   4.0". Write no copy that suggests partnership. Consider a courtesy email to the site's contact
   address. Never use the one-word "OpenBible".
4. **Ids:** fix `io.github.inhesrom.openbible` (or a domain-based id, if a domain is bought
   **before** the first upload) for iOS and Android. Reserve it early: create the App ID, the
   Play app and the Android Developer Console package registration together.
5. **Signing:** decide before the first Play release whether to upload our own app signing key
   to Play, so the GitHub APK and the Play build can update each other.

## Open questions for the decision ticket

1. Is the brand "Open Bible" or "Open Bible Software"? If trademark counsel advises against
   "Open Bible", what is the fallback name?
2. Will the project buy a domain (and which) before the first upload, or commit to
   `io.github.inhesrom.openbible` permanently?
3. Will the desktop app share the same id (Flatpak/macOS), or use `….openbiblesoftware` for
   desktop and `….openbible` for mobile?
4. Play App Signing: upload our own key (one key for Play and GitHub APK) or let Google generate
   it (two incompatible channels)?
5. Individual or organisation developer accounts? Organisation accounts need a verified website
   for Android developer verification and change the seller name shown on both stores.
6. Should we contact OpenBible.info and Thomas Nelson/HarperCollins in advance, or proceed
   without contact?
