FIELD DESCRIPTIONS=						>
==============
Alphabetical for the Unique name within the categories PERSON, PLACE, PLACE+PERSON, OTHER.
Records are separated by "$" and sub-records are on separate lines that start with a space.
(?) indicates  a decision based on an ambiguity e.g. Jahdai@1Ch.2.47(?) which may be discussed at	TinyURL.com/TIPNR-Decisions
(d) is added to a 'parent' when a group or nation Descended from them, EG Canaan@Gen.9.18-1Ch(d) is the parent of the Amorites   
(a) is added to a 'parent' who is an Ancestor of the person rather than their actual parent.  EG Mahol@1Ki.4.31=H4235 who is the ancestor of Zerah@Gen.38.30-Mat(a)  
(f) indicates a founder of that place or group, eg  "Tekoa@2Sa.14.2-Amo(f)
