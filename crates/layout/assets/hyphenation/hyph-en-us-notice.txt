Hyphenation patterns for American English (hyph-en-us.tex, 2005-05-30), part of the
hyph-utf8 package (https://www.hyphenation.org/tex), as compiled into the hypher crate
(https://github.com/typst/hypher, tries/en.bin) that Justified text uses to hyphenate
English words.

The patterns are Donald E. Knuth's hyphen.tex patterns for Plain TeX with Gerard D.C.
Kuiken's additions (ushyphmax.tex), based on the Hyphenation Exception Log published in
TUGboat, Volume 10 (1989), No. 3, pp. 337-341.
