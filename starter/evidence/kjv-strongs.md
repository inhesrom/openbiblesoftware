> Public Domain … Letters patent issued by King James with no expiration date means that to print this translation in the United Kingdom or import printed copies into the UK, you need permission. … This royal decree has no effect outside of the UK, where this work is firmly in the Public Domain. … You may copy the King James Version of the Holy Bible freely.

SPDX: `LicenseRef-PublicDomain`.

## Open question carried forward

The Crown letters patent cover *printing* in, or importing printed copies into, the UK. A digital app is not printing, but no lawyer has confirmed UK digital distribution ([open content inventory](../../docs/research/open-content-inventory.md), "KJV caveat"). The CrossWire `KJV` module (GPL-labelled) is not used.
