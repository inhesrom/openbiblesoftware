## Licence

Every page of the edition carries the banner:

> This expanded electronic edition of the J-F-B Commentary is in the public domain.

The "About the Electronic Edition" page says:

> This electronic edition of Jamieson, Fausset, and Brown's one-volume Commentary on the Whole Bible has been prepared from text scanned by Woodside Bible Fellowship. …
>
> Public Domain Status. This expanded edition of the Jameison-Faussett-Brown Commentary is in the public domain and may be freely used and distributed. It was first posted on the Christian Classics Ethereal Library server, at Wheaton College, in December 1997. The links to the Bible Gateway for Scripture references in that copy were added in January 1998 by Harry Plantinga.

SPDX: `LicenseRef-PublicDomain`. The printed work dates from 1871. The electronic edition's editor dedicates his edition to the public domain in the notice above, which ships inside the pinned zip.
