# Evidence: Matthew Henry's Commentary on the Whole Bible (`mhc`)

- **Edition shipped:** the English Wikisource transcription of Matthew Henry, *An Exposition of the Old and New Testament* (1706–1721; Romans to Revelation completed from Henry's notes by other ministers after his death), titled on Wikisource "Matthew Henry's Commentary on the Whole Bible". It is 134 pages under that title: six volumes, all 66 Bible books. The pinned revisions are the latest as of 2026-11-03 (revision ids 763514 to 12864246, last edited 2022-12-23).
- **Pinned file:** <https://web.archive.org/web/20261103225156id_/https://en.wikisource.org/w/api.php?action=query&generator=allpages&gapprefix=Matthew+Henry%27s+Commentary+on+the+Whole+Bible&gaplimit=max&export=1&exportnowrap=1> (Internet Archive capture, 2026-11-03, of the MediaWiki XML export of all 134 pages). The Internet Archive serves it gzip-compressed (`Content-Encoding: gzip`), and the pinned bytes are those gzip bytes, 13,951,385 bytes.
- **sha256:** `149a9a9fe7bfef10ab4d3ae99662051fe90a19243e53735ce85f323db30e0934`

## Licence

The main page carries Wikisource's `{{PD-old}}` licence tag, which renders as:

> This work was published before January 1, 1931, and is in the public domain worldwide because the author died at least 100 years ago.

Matthew Henry died in 1714; the volumes finished by others were published in 1721. SPDX: `LicenseRef-PublicDomain`.

## Where Wikisource got the text

The talk page records the source of this transcription:

> Information about this edition — Edition: 1708–1710 — Source: Christian Classics Ethereal Library (Calvin College)
>
> I understand from correspondence with the leading person at CCEL some years ago, that The Christian Classics Ethereal Library edition is from an unknown source and therefore cannot be further proofread.

So the words were first typed for CCEL; Wikisource re-marked them in wikitext from 2006 to 2022. We take no file from ccel.org: the pinned bytes are Wikisource's own export, served under Wikisource's public-domain tag. #20 decision 3 names this Wikisource transcription as the Matthew Henry source and sends CCEL a courtesy note. The CCEL origin is recorded as a risk in [docs/research/commentary-sources.md](../../docs/research/commentary-sources.md).
