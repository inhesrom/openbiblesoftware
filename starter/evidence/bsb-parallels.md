## What is kept

1,324 sections with 2,032 parallel Passages (build of 2026-11-07): every heading followed by a `\r` line, its verses, and its references as written. Dropped: one reference spanning Bible books (`Joshua–Malachi` beside Hebrews 11:30-40). The headings and references are kept verbatim, so the Berean name stays with them (the terms ask that it not be used only for works that vary from the official text).
